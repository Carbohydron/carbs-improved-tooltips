# Carbs Improved Tooltips

## Unreleased

- Also shortens the stats of the item a recipe makes, in recipe and profession tooltips.

## v1.0.0

First public release for World of Warcraft: Forever.

- Shortens spell damage and healing lines on item tooltips to "+X Spell Power" and
  "+X Heal Power".
- Shows "Equip: +X Attack Power." as "+X Attack Power".
- Shows "Equip: +X Attack Power against Y." as "+X Attack Power against Y".
- Shows "Equip: +X Ranged Attack Power." as "+X Ranged Attack Power".
- Shows "Equip: Improves your chance to hit by X%." as "+X% Hit".
- Shows "Equip: Increased Defense +X." as "+X Defense".
- Shows "Equip: Restores X mana per 5 sec." as "+X Mana per 5 sec.".
- Shows "Equip: Restores X health per 5 sec." as "+X Health per 5 sec.".
