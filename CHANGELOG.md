# Carbs Improved Tooltips

## v1.0.3

- Renames "Spell Power" to "Spell Damage" and "Heal Power" to "Spell Healing", matching
  the default item comparison tooltip.

## v1.0.2

- Shows "Equip: Improves your chance to get a critical strike by X%." as
  "+X% Critical Strike chance".
- Shows "Equip: Increases your chance to dodge an attack by X%." as "+X% Dodge".
- Shows weapon skill lines such as "Equip: Increased Two-Handed Axes +X." as
  "+X Two-Handed Axes skill", for any weapon type.
- Defense lines now show as "+X Defense skill", matching weapon skills.
- Also shortens the stats of the item a profession recipe makes, in the profession window,
  spellbook and recipe links.

## v1.0.0

First public release for World of Warcraft: Forever.

- Shortens spell damage and healing lines on item tooltips to "+X Spell Power" and
  "+X Heal Power".
- Shows "Equip: +X Attack Power." as "+X Attack Power".
- Shows "Equip: +X Attack Power against Y." as "+X Attack Power against Y".
- Shows "Equip: +X Ranged Attack Power." as "+X Ranged Attack Power".
- Shows "Equip: Improves your chance to hit by X%." as "+X% Hit".
- Shows "Equip: Increased Defense +X." as "+X Defense".
- Shows "Equip: Restores X mana per 5 sec." as "+X Mana per 5 sec.".
- Shows "Equip: Restores X health per 5 sec." as "+X Health per 5 sec.".
